Processing

Orders placed before 2 pm (local warehouse time) on a business day ship the same day. Later orders ship the next business day. You'll get an email with tracking as soon as your parcel leaves.

International

We ship to more than 40 countries, including Vietnam, Singapore, Japan, Australia, Canada, the UK and most of the EU.

  • International standard: 5–10 business days.
  • Any duties and taxes are calculated and shown at checkout, so there are no surprises on delivery.
  • Free international shipping applies to orders over $150.

Tracking

Every order is tracked. Use the link in your shipping email, or contact us with your order number and we'll check on it for you.

Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel hasn't arrived 5 business days after its estimated date, or arrives damaged, tell us within 14 days and we'll send a replacement at no cost.
