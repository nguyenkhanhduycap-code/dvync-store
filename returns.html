How to return

  • Go to Contact us and choose "Return or refund", including your order number.
  • We'll email a prepaid return label within one business day.
  • Pack the items securely, attach the label and drop the parcel at any carrier location.

Refunds

We refund to your original payment method within 3 business days of your return arriving. Your bank may take a further 5–10 business days to show it. Original shipping costs are refunded if the item was faulty or we sent the wrong thing.

Memberships

If you cancel within your free month, return the band and you won't be charged. After that, an annual membership can be cancelled within 30 days of renewal for a prorated refund.

Exchanges

Need a different band size or colour? Exchanges are free within 30 days. We ship the new item as soon as your return is scanned by the carrier.

Warranty

All hardware carries a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Active members get free sensor replacements for the whole time their membership is active.

Questions about a return? Contact our team. We reply within one business day.
