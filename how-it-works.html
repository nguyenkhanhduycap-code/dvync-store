What D'Vync measures

Sleep, measured properly

D'Vync detects light, deep and REM sleep, plus time awake, from your heart rate and movement. No buttons and nothing to log.

Your sleep need starts from your own baseline, then adds time for today's strain and any sleep debt, and takes off time for naps.

How well you slept matters as much as how long. One score weighs the hours you got against the hours you needed, along with consistency, efficiency and wake-ups.

Choose a goal and a wake time. The sleep planner suggests when to get into bed, and a silent vibration wakes you without disturbing anyone else.

Look back over a week, a month or a year to spot patterns and build a routine that holds.

Train with a purpose

Strain adds up the load on your heart and muscles across the whole day, from a hard session to a long walk, on a scale from 0 to 21.

See how long you spent in each zone, so easy days stay easy and hard days are hard enough.

Each morning your coach suggests a workout and a strain target matched to how recovered you are.

Strength sessions are split into cardio and muscular load, and each one is compared with your past workouts.

Recover on purpose

Every morning you get a score from 0 to 100%. Green means go, yellow means steady, red means take it easy today.

Heart rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate and sleep all feed in, and the app tells you which one moved your score.

A week at a glance shows how training, travel and late nights add up, long before you'd feel it.

Stay ahead of stress

A simple 0–3 scale shows how stressed your body is right now, based on your heart rate and heart rate variability.

Guided breathing sessions help you calm down or wake up, and you can watch your heart respond as you go.

Find exactly when stress climbed during the day and connect it to meetings, commutes or caffeine.

Habits that stick

Answer a few quick questions each morning about caffeine, alcohol, screens or late meals. After a few weeks you'll see exactly how much each habit helps or hurts your recovery.

Your vitals, at a glance

Heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, blood oxygen and skin temperature in one place, with a flag when anything moves outside your normal range.

Stay close to your heart health

Each reading is checked for signs of an irregular rhythm, and any report can be exported as a PDF in one tap.

D'Vync flags heart rates that stay unusually high or low while you rest. It isn't a medical device, but it helps you start the right conversation.

Add healthy years, not just years

Healthspan looks at the key signals from your sleep, fitness and heart to estimate your D'Vync Age, the age your body is actually performing at, and how fast it's changing. Each month you'll see which habits will move it most.

Questions, answered.

Do I have to wear it all the time?

The more you wear it, the more accurate it gets. Sleep is the most important time to wear it, because that's when recovery is measured.

How do I charge it without taking it off?

Slide the battery pack onto the band while you wear it. It charges in about 90 minutes, and you never lose data.

Does it work without my phone nearby?

Yes. The band stores several days of data and syncs as soon as your phone is back in range.

Which phones does it work with?

iPhone and Android phones from the last five years. It also connects to popular training apps.

Is it a medical device?

No. D'Vync is a wellness product and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a healthcare professional about medical concerns.
