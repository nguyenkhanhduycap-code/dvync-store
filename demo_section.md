### Script

[Slide 16 – Live demo: D’Vync website prototype. Browser ready on localhost, cart empty. Speaker’s laptop, one tab per page – see the demo checklist.]

[9:00 – Transition in]

Every stage of that funnel leads to one place: our website. It has three jobs: communication, capturing leads, and conversion. Let me show you how it does all three.

[9:10 – Customer journey and homepage]

[SWITCH: to the browser, homepage tab] Imagine I watched a creator’s daily routine video on TikTok and tapped the link. [PAUSE – page loads] I land on the homepage, and it must pass the Grunt Test in seconds. What is it? [POINT: hero headline “The proven wearable for better health and performance”] A wearable for better health and performance. How does it help me? [POINT: “Know your body every day, and start your first month free.”] I can know my body every day, and the first month is free. What do I do next? [POINT: “Join now” button] One clear button: Join now. That is communication: the page explains itself. [SCROLL: down to the 01 Wear / 02 Learn / 03 Improve steps] Below, three steps sum it up: Wear, Learn, Improve.

[9:48 – Site hierarchy]

[POINT: header menu – Memberships, Accessories, How it works, Why D’Vync?, Blog, Help] Now notice the menu: only six items. That keeps the site simple and scalable, and every main page stays within three clicks of the homepage.

[10:00 – Design]

[POINT: header logo – circle with lime dot] The design follows our brand kit: a circle logo, black, off-white and one lime accent, Barlow Condensed for headings and Montserrat for everything else, [SCROLL: down to the “Made to be worn. Made to be forgotten.” section] and our tagline, “Made to be worn. Made to be forgotten.” Sporty for athletes, calm for minimalists.

[10:13 – Key pages and the path to checkout]

Let’s follow the journey. [CLICK: How it works] [PAUSE – page loads] One click, and How it works explains the band in plain words. [CLICK: Memberships ▾ → D’Vync Peak] [PAUSE – page loads] Two clicks, and I reach the Peak membership, [SCROLL: to the plan comparison table – One / Peak / Life / Free month] with plans and the free first month. [SCROLL: show the floating “Join now” button] As I scroll, the Join now button follows me. I click it, [CLICK: Join now] then View cart, [CLICK: View cart in the pop-up – it disappears after about 3 seconds; if it is gone, click the cart icon in the header] then Checkout. [CLICK: Checkout] [PAUSE – page loads] Just three steps from plan to payment. [POINT: “Demo checkout” banner] The form is short: contact, address, delivery and payment. [FILL: email, phone, first and last name, address, city, region, postal code, country = Vietnam (the list starts with United States, so pick it); then test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, expiry 12/30, CVC 123 – keep talking while you type] This is capturing: we collect the customer's details. It is a demo, so I use the test card and no real money moves. [CLICK: Place order] [PAUSE – about 2 seconds, the thank-you page loads] And here is the thank-you page, with my order number: conversion, a visitor becomes a customer. [POINT: “Thank you for your order!” and the order number]

[10:51 – Pricing note]

[POINT: total on the thank-you page] Prices are in dong. The cheapest plan, One, is our Part 2 Premium price: 990,000 dong a year. The others are samples.

[10:59 – Product page, trust pages, mobile]

We also sell accessories. [CLICK: Accessories] [SCROLL: the “New arrivals” row sideways with the arrow button to the last card] [CLICK: Halo Mesh Band – Rose Gold] [PAUSE – page loads] Two clicks take me to a product page, with photos, an Add to cart button, the stock level, reviews, an FAQ, and shipping and returns. [POINT: gallery, “Add to cart”, “Only 6 left in stock”; OPEN accordions: Shipping, FAQ, Reviews] The reviews are samples for now. [SCROLL: to footer – Shipping, Returns & refunds, Health data privacy, Contact us] Our trust pages sit in the footer. And since many visitors arrive on their phones, the site works on mobile too. [SWITCH: mobile view in browser developer tools, 2 seconds, then back]


[11:51 – Infrastructure]

[NO CLICKS – stay on the page, look at the audience] A quick word on infrastructure. Today this runs as a local prototype: no live domain yet. At launch we need four things: a domain name, web hosting, this same software, and tools like a payment gateway and analytics.

[12:05 – Why our website is stronger]

[NO CLICKS – stay on the page, look at the audience] One point makes our website stronger: its design. Many shop sites interrupt you with pop-ups and flashing banners. Ours stays calm, just like our band: lots of space, big photos, and no pop-up unless you click. A site that never interrupts you, for a band that never interrupts you.

[12:24 – Transition out]

From a TikTok video to checkout, in just a few clicks. Now let me bring everything together. [SWITCH: back to slides]
