Membership

What's included in a membership?

The D'Vync band, a knit strap, a charger and full access to the app for your plan's term. Your band is replaced free if it fails while you're a member.

How does the free month work?

Your first 30 days are free. If you cancel before day 31 and return the band, you pay nothing. Otherwise your annual membership begins automatically.

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Upgrades take effect immediately and you only pay the prorated difference. Downgrades apply at your next renewal.

Can I pay with HSA or FSA funds?

In the US, many plans accept D'Vync memberships. Choose your HSA/FSA card at checkout, and keep your receipt for reimbursement.

Band & hardware

How long does the battery last?

About five days per charge with typical use. The Power Pack lets you charge without taking the band off, so you never lose a night of sleep data.

Is it waterproof?

Yes, it's water-resistant to 10 m. You can swim and shower with it. Rinse with fresh water after the ocean or a pool.

What size band do I need?

Every band is one size fits all. The adjustable fit suits wrists from about 13 to 21 cm, so you never need to choose a size.

Why doesn't it have a screen?

No screen means fewer distractions, a lighter band and a battery that lasts days instead of hours. Everything you need is waiting in the app.

Shipping

How much does shipping cost?

Standard shipping is free on orders over $75 and always free for memberships. Otherwise it's $6.95, or $14.95 for express. See the shipping policy.

When will my order arrive?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days and express takes 1–2. Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to more than 40 countries, including Vietnam, Singapore, Japan, Australia, the UK and most of the EU. Duties are shown at checkout.

Returns & warranty

What is your return policy?

You can return anything within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, even if you've worn it. Read the full returns policy.

How long do refunds take?

Once your return arrives, we refund within 3 business days. Your bank may take another 5–10 days to show it.

What does the warranty cover?

Hardware has a 1-year limited warranty against defects. Active members get free replacements for as long as their membership lasts.

Data & privacy

Do you sell my health data?

Never. Your data is used only to provide your insights, and you can export or permanently delete it from the app at any time.

Where is my data stored?

Encrypted in transit and at rest on secure cloud infrastructure. You control which third-party apps can read it. Read our full health data privacy policy.

Advanced Labs

What is Advanced Labs?

An add-on that lets you order a blood panel and see your biomarkers next to your sleep, strain and recovery data in the app.

How do I get tested?

Book a draw at a partner lab near you from the app. Results usually arrive within 3–5 days and come with plain-language explanations.
