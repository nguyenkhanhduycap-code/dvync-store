What we collect

We only collect what we need to give you accurate insights and run your membership.

Type of dataExamplesWhy we need it
Sensor dataHeart rate, HRV, breathing rate, skin temperature, blood oxygen, movementTo calculate sleep, recovery, strain and stress
Health featuresECG readings, blood pressure estimates, cycle and pregnancy logsOnly if you turn these features on
ProfileAge, height, weight, sex, goalsTo personalise your scores and targets
Journal entriesCaffeine, alcohol, sleep habits you choose to logTo show which habits affect your recovery
Account & ordersName, email, address, order historyTo ship your band and manage your membership
App diagnosticsCrash reports, app version, device modelTo fix bugs and keep the app stable

We don't collect your precise location unless you record a GPS workout, and we never access your contacts, photos or messages.

How we use it

  • To calculate your daily scores, trends and coaching.
  • To power optional features such as Healthspan, heart screening and blood pressure insights.
  • To provide support when you ask us for help.
  • To improve our algorithms using de-identified data, only if you allow it (see Research).

We do not use your health data for advertising, and we never build marketing profiles from it.

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal or health data. We only share it in these limited cases:

  • Apps you connect. If you link Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Strava or another app, we send only the data types you approve. You can disconnect at any time.
  • People you choose. For example, when you export an ECG report as a PDF to share with your doctor, or join a team with friends.
  • Service providers. Trusted companies that host our servers, deliver orders or process payments, under strict contracts that forbid any other use.
  • Legal requirements. Only when we're legally required to, and we'll tell you unless the law prevents us.

How we protect it

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Health data is stored separately from your name and email.
  • Only a small number of trained staff can access member data, only to resolve a support request, and every access is logged.
  • We run regular independent security audits and a public bug bounty programme.

Your controls

Everything below is available in the app under Settings → Privacy:

  • See all the data we hold about you.
  • Export a full copy as CSV and PDF, ready within 48 hours.
  • Turn off optional features and research sharing at any time.
  • Delete individual records, or your entire account and all its data.

You can also make any of these requests through our contact page. We respond within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active so your trends stay complete. If you delete your account, we erase your personal and health data within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days. Order records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

Research

Research helps us make D'Vync more accurate. Taking part is always optional and off by default. If you opt in, we only use de-identified data, and you can opt out at any time without affecting your membership.

Children

D'Vync memberships are for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 16; if we learn that we have, we delete it.

Changes & contact

If we make a meaningful change to this policy, we'll tell you in the app and by email before it takes effect.
